# Requirements Checklist

Use this after every client call. If you cannot tick a box, that is your next question to the client.

**Project:** [name]
**Client contact:** [name]
**Date:** [date]
**Written by:** [your name]

## 1. The goal
- [ ] What problem are we solving, in one sentence?
- [ ] Why does the client want to solve it now?
- [ ] How will we know the project worked? (one or two measurable results)

## 2. The users
- [ ] Who will use the product? (list each type of user)
- [ ] What does each type of user need to do?
- [ ] Who is not a user, but still needs to approve things?

## 3. The scope
- [ ] What must work on day one? (must-have)
- [ ] What would be nice to have later? (nice-to-have)
- [ ] What is clearly NOT included?

## 4. The details
- [ ] Platforms: web, iOS, Android, or all?
- [ ] Other systems it must connect to (payments, email, CRM, APIs)
- [ ] Existing designs, brand rules or documents to follow
- [ ] Data that must be moved from an old system
- [ ] Security or legal rules to follow

## 5. Time and money
- [ ] Hard deadline? What is it tied to? (event, contract, season)
- [ ] Budget range
- [ ] Who gives the final approval?

## 6. How we will work
- [ ] How often do we update the client? (weekly is a good start)
- [ ] Who answers questions on the client side?
- [ ] How do we handle change requests?
