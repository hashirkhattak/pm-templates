## How to score
- **Chance:** 1 (unlikely) to 5 (very likely)
- **Impact:** 1 (small) to 5 (project fails)
- **Score:** Chance x Impact. Act first on 15 and above.

## Common risks to think about
- Unclear or changing requirements
- Late client feedback or approvals
- Unrealistic deadline
- Team members leaving or on leave
- Dependency on another team or vendor
- New technology the team has not used before
- Test environment or data not ready
- Payment, security or legal checks at the end
