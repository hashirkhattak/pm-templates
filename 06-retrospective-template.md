# Sprint Retrospective

**Sprint:** [number or name]
**Date:** [date]
**Attendees:** [names]
**Time needed:** 45 to 60 minutes

## Ground rules
- We talk about the work and the process, not about blaming people.
- Everyone speaks. Quiet people go first.
- We leave with a small number of real actions.

## Step 1: What happened? (10 min)
Look at the sprint goal and the board.
- Did we meet the sprint goal? [yes / partly / no]
- Planned vs finished: [e.g. 12 stories planned, 10 finished]
- What surprised us?

## Step 2: Three columns (15 min)
Each person adds notes. Group similar ones.

| Went well | Did not go well | Ideas to try |
| --- | --- | --- |
| | | |

## Step 3: Pick the top problems (10 min)
Vote. Each person gets 3 votes. Pick the top 2 or 3.

## Step 4: Actions (15 min)
Every action has one owner and a date.

| Action | Owner | Due |
| --- | --- | --- |
| [small, specific change] | [name] | [date] |

## Step 5: Follow up
- [ ] Actions added to the board for next sprint
- [ ] Last sprint's actions reviewed: done / not done / dropped
- [ ] Summary shared with the team

## Good actions vs weak actions
- Weak: "Communicate better."
- Good: "Post blockers in the team channel by 11 am. Owner: [name]."
